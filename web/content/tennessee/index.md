The national numbers and the TVA numbers describe a shape. This is where that shape lands on actual ground, in four Tennessee places where something is already built, already proposed, or already contracted.

Each of these is documented separately because each raises a different question. Gallatin is about what a claim means when you check it. Memphis is about what happens when the permits are skipped. Nashville is about who gets asked before an infrastructure decision is made. Hendersonville is about the data collection that arrives years before anyone proposes a data center.

- [Meta Gallatin](meta_gallatin/). The "100% solar" claim, what the facility actually draws, and what Gallatin traded for it.
- [xAI Memphis](xai_memphis/). Unpermitted gas turbines next to Boxtown, and the clearest documented harm in the region.
- [Fisk University](fisk/). A 30 MW data center proposed in North Nashville, and the I-40 parallel neighbors keep raising.
- [Hendersonville and Placer.ai](hendersonville/). The city contracted to track cell phone movement. It is in their own public minutes.
