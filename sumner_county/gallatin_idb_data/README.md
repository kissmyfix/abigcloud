# sumner_county/gallatin_idb_data/

## Purpose
Official meeting records of the Industrial Development Board of the City of Gallatin, Tennessee — the entity at the center of the investigation.

## Contents
Meeting minutes and agendas.

## Source Type
**Primary Source**

The IDB's own official documentation of its deliberations and decisions.

## Handling Instructions
- Cite by meeting date and document type (minutes vs. agenda)
- Cross-reference dates against the SOS filing history (`1994-2026-idb-gallatin.pdf` in this directory; key dates also in `memory/MEMORY.md`) — gaps during key transition periods are themselves findings:
  - December 2012 (dissolution)
  - February 2013 (reinstatement with Bradford)
  - July 2018 (Fenton moves IDB from City Attorney to GEDA)
  - July 2021 (Stark installed, Woolhawk signing window)
- When records are absent for periods that should show activity, flag the absence explicitly — do not treat missing records as neutral
- Note whether Bradford Company, Beretta, or Woolhawk appear in minutes and when each name first and last appears

## Notes
Absence of records is data in this investigation. The IDB's pattern of governance failures means gaps in meeting minutes are unlikely to be accidental. The board's own disclosure that it does not review 990 filings before submission should be held against what the minutes do and don't reflect.
