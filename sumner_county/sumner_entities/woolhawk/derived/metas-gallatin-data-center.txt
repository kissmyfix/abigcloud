Partnering with Tennessee
We are committed to supporting the community through
sourcing labor and materials locally where we can, volunteering,
partnering with local chambers, and supporting local schools,
nonprofits and community projects.



       Supporting local schools and nonprofits
       We support the community through our annual Data
       Center Community Action Grants program and other
       direct funding for projects that put the power of
       technology to use for community benefit, give people
       the power to build strong, sustainable communities,
       and improve STEAM education.
