# sumner_county/

## Purpose
Everything chartered or recorded at the city/county level — the local paper trail of the Gallatin IDB, the city bodies around it, and the corporations holding PILOT agreements.

## Contents
- `gallatin_idb_data/` — IDB meeting minutes and agendas
- `gallatin_council_meetings/` — public documents from gallatintn.gov tied to the IDB, entities, and the_players
- `gallatin_electric_utility/` — GDE service policies and rate sheets (2016–present)
- `sumner_entities/` — one directory per PILOT-holding corporation (11 tracked), each with its own `memory/MEMORY.md` as source of truth for the tracker site

## Source Type
Mixed — the `gallatin_*` directories are **Primary Source** (official local government records); `sumner_entities/` is **Working Material** (our per-entity analysis, always traced back to primary sources).

## Handling Instructions
- Read each subdirectory's own README before touching its files
- Local records here are the ground truth for what the city and IDB actually did and said in public — cross-reference against the state filings (`state_of_tennessee/`) and federal filings (`usa_federal/`) for the contradiction work

## Notes
The city-only charter of the Gallatin IDB (not joint with Sumner County) is what makes the local/county boundary legally significant — see `state_of_tennessee/README.md`, checklist item 7.
