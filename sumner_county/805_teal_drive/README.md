## What the chain establishes

1. **9/20/2021** — the Blacks buy it for **$1.2M**, exactly as the *Hendersonville Standard*
   reported from Sumner County tax records.
2. **10/19/2021** — conveyed for **$0**, a limited-qualification transfer: the gift to CAP.
   Four weeks before the 2021-11-13 gala where it was announced.
3. **Feb 2022** — CAP, as owner, obtains **CUP-2022-0012** to convert it to a **100-child daycare**
   (Gallatin Municipal BZA, 2026-02-24 packet).
4. **1/12/2026** — **CAP sells it for $1,200,000** to **Keiths Canines Holdings LLC** — an
   arm's-length warranty deed, at **$414,000 above the 2026 assessed market value of $786,000**,
   and at exactly the price the Blacks paid four years earlier.

## Why this is the centre of the CAP story

The sale is **six months before** CAP's Articles of Dissolution (2026-06-10) and **five months
before** the press announced a "$1.5 million legacy endowment, principal protected" going to
Gallatin Shalom Zone (2026-06-16).

**The endowment is the building, liquidated.** CAP's largest asset was the donated real property;
it converted it to $1.2M cash in January 2026 and dissolved. Everything in the sequence lines up:

    2026-01-05  Notice of Determination — delinquent annual report
    2026-01-06  2025 annual report filed; "Officers Changed"; mailing address off the PO box
    2026-01-12  BUILDING SOLD, $1,200,000
    2026-01-22  registered agent address moved OFF 805 Teal Drive to 797 Plantation Blvd

The sale falls in **FYE 2026-06-30** — so CAP's FY2026 return, due autumn 2026, must show both the
$1.2M disposition and the distribution, on **Schedule N**. That single return now answers the
whole question.

Source: `inbox/SOS-keiths-canines-holdings-llc.pdf` (TN SOS), filed 2026-08-28.

- **Control Number 000938053**, Tennessee LLC, **Director Managed**, **3 members**
- **Initial filing 2017-12-27**, effective 2018-01-01 — **formed eight years before the purchase**,
  not created for it
- **Registered agent, principal office and mailing address: BERNARD WINFRED KEITH, JR,
  1038 Stanley Dr, Mt Juliet, TN 37122** — Wilson County, not Sumner
- Status **Active**; AR standing good; **Revenue Standing: NOT GOOD** (a Dept. of Revenue matter)
- Thirteen history entries, all routine: annual reports, two delinquency notices (2019, 2023),
  system amendments. Nothing referencing the acquisition — LLCs do not report property purchases.

**What this does and does not establish.** On its face the buyer is an ordinary outside party: a
pre-existing Mt Juliet dog-boarding/kennel holding company with no visible tie to CAP's board, to
the Blacks, or to the Gallatin cluster, buying a building whose IR–Industrial Restrictive zoning
suits a kennel. The assessor coded the sale **A — ACCEPTED**, i.e. arm's length.

**Tennessee does not require LLC members to be named in this record, and the three members are not
disclosed here.** So this document cannot exclude a relationship; it simply shows none. The deed
itself (**6764/167**) and any related filings are where that gets settled.

**The live question is therefore not who bought it but where the $1,200,000 went** — and whether the
sale price, $414,000 over the county's $786,000 appraisal and exactly the 2021 purchase price, is
explained anywhere.
