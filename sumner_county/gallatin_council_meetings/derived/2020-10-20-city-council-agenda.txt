                                                                  RESOLUTION NO. R20II.6O



RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SUMNER COUNTY GOVERNMENT TO CONSTRUCT
AN ADDITION TO FIRE STATION #3, 860 RED RIVER ROAD FOR THE PURPOSE OF
HOUSING AN AMBULANCE AND EMS PERSONNEL




        WHEREAS, the City of Gallatin Planning Commission approved GMRPC Resolution

No. 2020-081 on 511812020 approving a revised Final Master Development Plan for Gallatin Fire

Station #3; and

       WHEREAS, Sumner County EMS requested the revision for the purpose of adding an

ambulance bay and EMS staff sleeping quarters to Fire Station #3; and

       WHEREAS, Sumner County Government would be responsible for all costs and

construction liability associated with requested addition to Fire Station #3, and

       WHEREAS, it is in the best interests of the residents of the City of Gallatin to participate

with Sumner CountY.

       NOW THERE,FORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY OF GALLATIN'
TENNESSEE, that the city resolves the county is authorized to construct an ambulance bay and
                                                                                                  for the
EMS staff sleeping quarters at Fire Station #3, and other building improvements required

addition,


       BE IT F,URTHER RESOLVED that this resolution shall take effect from and after its

final passage by the City Council, the public welfare requiring such.
