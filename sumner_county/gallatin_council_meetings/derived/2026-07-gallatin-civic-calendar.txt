WHY SAVED: It is the only document in the archive showing when the Industrial
  Development Board meets, and it shows the IDB does not have a slot of its own.

  The single entry, Wednesday 8 July 2026:

      "Industrial Development Board & Health, Educational, and Housing Board"

  One calendar item, two boards, the same nine directors. Every other body on the
  month has its own line: City Court, Planning Commission Work Session, City
  Council Meeting, Economic Development Agency, Beer Board, Board of Zoning
  Appeals, City Council Committee Meeting.

WHAT IT DOES NOT SHOW: any time of day, and any month other than July 2026. An
  earlier draft of the article said the IDB "shares its Monday 4:30 PM slot with
  one other board." The day is **Wednesday** on this calendar and no time appears
  anywhere on it. Do not restate the Monday or the 4:30 without another source.
