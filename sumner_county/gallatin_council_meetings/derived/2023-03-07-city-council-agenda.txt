                                               CITY OF GALLATIN
                                               COUNCIL MEETING
 March 07, 2023                                         6:00 pm                          Dr. J. Deotha Malone
                                                                                          Council Chambers

 • Call to Order - Mayor Brown
 • Invocation
 • Pledge of Allegiance- Councilman Hayes
 • Roll Call: Carter - Fann - Fennell - George - Hayes - Jouvence - Vice Mayor Love
 • Approval of Minutes: February 07, 2023 City Council Meeting
 • Public Recognition on Agenda-Related Items
 • Mayor's Comments


                                                     AGENDA
1. Public Hearing Ordinance No. 02302-17 An ordinance of the City of Gallatin, Sumner County, Tennessee,
   approving an amendment to the Preliminary Master Development Plan for Goodview Commons (F.K.A. Maddux
   Commercial Park) on a portion of a 3.16 +/- acre lot, located west of GreenLea Boulevard and south of SR386;
   authorizing the revision to be indicated on the Official Zoning Atlas; repealing conflicting ordinances; providing for
   severability, and providing for an effective date. (Councilman Jouvence)

2. Public Hearing Ordinance No. 02302-18 An ordinance of the City of Gallatin, Sumner County, Tennessee,
   approving an Amended Preliminary Master Development Plan and Revised Final Master Development Plan for
   Enoch Hills, Phase 3 on a 30.88 +/- acre parcel, located north of Hartsville Pike and west of Woodlands Drive,
   authorizing the revision to be indicated on the Official Zoning Atlas; repealing conflicting ordinances, providing for
   severability, and providing for an effective date. (Vice Mayor Love)

3. Second Reading Ordinance No. 02212-71 An ordinance of the City of Gallatin, Sumner County, Tennessee,
   rezoning 23.28 +/- acres from the R-15 Medium Density Residential District to the MRO-Multiple Residential and
   Office District and 2.00+/- acres from the R-10 Medium Density Residential District to the MRO-Multiple
   Residential and Office District with a Preliminary Master Development Plan for Laurelwood Park, located south of
   Drivers Lane and east of Lorraine Drive; authorizing the revision to be indicated on the Official Zoning Atlas;
   repealing conflicting ordinances; providing for severability, and providing for an effective date. (Councilwoman
   George)

4.   Second Reading Ordinance No. 02302-15 Ordinance appropriating $7,575.40 for insurance recovery from fire
     suppression operations at 155 East Main Street (new courthouse under construction) on August 7, 2022.
     (Councilman Carter)

5. Second Reading Ordinance No. 02302-16 Ordinance appropriating $3,500.00 from Project Woolhawk for
   Planning Department office renovation expenses (Councilman Hayes)

6. Second Reading Ordinance No. 02302-19 Ordinance amending Gallatin Municipal Code Chapter 2, Article I,
   Section 2-5 Acceptance of Gratuities (Councilwoman George)

7. First Reading Ordinance No. 02303-20 Ordinance appropriating $1,400,000.00 for construction of the City of
   Gallatin Police Department Gun Range Facility (Councilman Fennell)
