The property is also included in the Nashville Pike Corridor Plan, which was adopted by the

DISCUSSION:
Proposed Development
Owner and applicant requests approval of an Ordinance to rezone a 0.67 +/- acre lot from the R-40
Low Density Residential District to the MRO-Multiple Residential and Office District with a
Preliminary Master Development Plan for the Montgomery Office, located south of Nashville Pike
and east of Lakeshore Drive. The Financial, Consulting, and Administrative (General Office) use is a
permitted use in the MRO-Multiple Residential and Office Zoning District.

The PMDP for the Montgomery Office includes an existing two-story 2,223 square foot residence to
be converted into an office space with adjacent parking. The existing driveway adjacent to the
residence in the side yard will be retained for this development.

The construction schedule anticipates construction occurring between July 2020 and December
2020.

Natural Features
This property contains many trees and an extensive canopy. Most of the trees will remain.

Based on FEMA FIRM Map 471650426G, dated of April 17, 2012, no portion of the site is located
within a special flood hazard area.

Adjacent or Area Uses
To the north and west are former residential homes, which have already been converted to office
space, zoned MRO-Multiple Residential and Office. A residence is located to the east. To the south
is Leeland Heights Lakefront, a residence subdivision, zoned R-40 Low Density Residential
(Attachment 6).

Gallatin on the Move 2020 General Development and Transportation Plan
The Gallatin on the Move 2020 General Development and Transportation Plan identifies this
property as a part of the Nashville Pike Office Corridor on the Community Character Map. This area
includes residences converting to office uses. The adjacent properties to the north, east and west are
also identified as a part of the Nashville Pike Office Corridor with the adjacent properties to the
south (Leeland Heights Lakefront) identified as the Suburban Neighborhood Established Character
Area. The proposed rezoning and PMDP is consistent with the Nashville Pike Office Corridor
Character Area. (Attachment 7)

Nashville Pike Office Corridor Character Area
The Nashville Pike Office Corridor is established along the south side of Nashville Pike from the
East Fork of Camp Creek to SR 109 and consists of former residences, most of which have
converted to office uses and neighborhood commercial uses mostly developed during the last ten
(10) years. While predominantly comprised of office/professional and neighborhood commercial
uses, the area west of Peninsula Drive retains a residential design which provides an attractive entry
gateway into the City located west of SR-109 Bypass.

   The intent of the Nashville Pike Office Corridor Character Area is:
     To provide for pedestrian friendly areas with public spaces, relatively high intensity mix of
     businesses, retail and office, employment, education, hospitality and entertainment
     facilities in a residential setting, while preserving the residential character of this gateway

Nashville Pike Corridor Plan – SR-109 to East Camp Creek
The Nashville Pike Corridor Plan was prepared and approved in response to increasing development
pressures and the rapid growth along the Nashville Pike Corridor from SR-109 to East Camp Creek
by the Planning Commission in 2000 as a General Development Plan Amendment. This plan is also
referenced in the Gallatin on the Move 2020 and incorporated there in. The City adopted policies to
balance future development requests to preserve and protect stable and established neighborhoods.

The General Development and Transportation Plan Amendment-Nashville Pike Corridor includes
policies that guide future planning program initiatives and applies new technologies and land use
strategies. The plan is not a static document; rather, it is designed to accommodate change.

The Nashville Pike Corridor Plan set guidelines for architectural standards recognizing residential
character of the Nashville Pike Corridor as one of the greatest assets of this area. This corridor serves
as a gateway into the heart of Gallatin. New development should be compatible in scale and form
with this character as well as with the character of the surrounding residential neighborhoods.
Relevant portions of the plan are provided in Attachment 8.

The Corridor Plan envisions that the area on the south side of Nashville Pike from Peninsula Drive
to East Camp Creek:

       “…will develop through the adaptive reuse of the existing residential structures. This Plan
       supports the conversion of the existing residences into non-residential uses. This will permit
       the development of the property while maintaining the unique character of this area. This
       Plan does not support the demolition of the existing structures. However, any new
       construction in this area should be designed to match the established residential character.”

The proposed rezoning and PMDP is consistent with the Nashville Pike Corridor Plan.

Access and Easements (P.U.D.E.)
The PMDP indicates that the existing driveway to Nashville Pike will be removed and a new
frontage road/access easement will be installed for access to the business from the adjacent property.
This configuration is consistent with the Nashville Pike Corridor Plan and the Nashville Pike Access
Management Plan (Attachment 9).

The PMDP also includes a public sidewalk to be installed along Nashville Pike and a private
sidewalk connection to be provided into the interior of the site.

The Nashville Pike Corridor Plan includes general bufferyard design types. The rear bufferyard shall
be revised to a Type 50 buffer to match the Nashville Pike Corridor Plan, although the zoning code
only requires a Type 35 buffer between these zoning districts. This should be achievable since there
is no proposed development between the existing house and the rear property boundary, and the
house is more than 50’ away from the rear property boundary.

The bufferyards include existing mature trees and will be supplemented with additional vegetation
where required to meet the bufferyard requirements.

A Final Landscaping plan will be submitted with the FMDP.

Parking
Required parking is based on the use of the property. General Office uses require a minimum of one
(1) space per 400 square feet and a maximum of one (1) space per 250 square feet. Therefore, a
minimum of six (6) parking spaces is required and a maximum of nine (9) parking spaces allowed.
The PMDP proposes nine (9) parking spaces, including one (1)) accessible space, which meets the
parking regulations as required by the G.Z.O.

Architectural Designs
The existing two-story residence, which includes a garage and a sunroom on the rear, will remain
and be converted into the office space (Attachment 10). Building modifications will be required to
meet commercial building code.

Master Signage Plan
The PMDP shows a monument sign to be located along Nashville Pike. A final sign package must be
submitted to the Planning Department for review and approval prior to the installation of any
signage on site. All signage installed on the site must comply with Section 13.07, Sign Regulation, of
the Gallatin Zoning Ordinance.

  RESOLUTION TO APPROVE PAYMENT IN LIEU OF TAX (PILOT) PROGRAM
    ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT INCENTIVE FOR PROJECT WOOLHAWK

       WHEREAS, the Industrial Development Board of the City of Gallatin (the
“Industrial Development Board”), is authorized under the provisions of Chapter 53, Title
7, of Tennessee Code Annotated, as amended from time to time (the "Act"), to, among
other things, acquire, improve, construct, expand, equip, own, lease, and dispose of
properties in order to promote industry, trade, commerce, tourism and recreation, and
thereby maintain and increase employment opportunities and further the use of natural
resources in the State of Tennessee, by inducing manufacturing, industrial, governmental,
educational, financial, service, commercial, and recreational enterprises to locate in or
remain in the State of Tennessee, and

       WHEREAS, the Industrial Development Board is currently working to bring
additional jobs and revenue to the City of Gallatin and Sumner County through Project
Woolhawk; and

        WHEREAS, it is sometimes necessary to entice such commercial enterprises with
financial incentives; and

        WHEREAS, the Industrial Development Board has proposed a PILOT program
incentive with Woolhawk, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Company”),
as follows (the “PILOT Program”):

           1. The Company shall make a payment in lieu of tax for the land that it leases
              from the Industrial Development Board equal to the amount of ad valorem
              taxes that would have been payable to each of the City and the County on
              the land for the tax year immediately prior to the date of the lease as zoned
              to permit such Limited Manufacturing, from the date of the lease to the date
              immediately prior to the date of completion of the first building (defined as
              a data center containing at least 155,000 square feet) constructed thereon.

           2. Each building constructed on the Project Woolhawk site and the equipment
              therein will receive a tax abatement for each of the first partial and next
              twenty (20) full tax years after the completion of the construction of the
              building, and the Company shall make a payment in lieu of tax for each
              building after completion as follows:

                  a. One Million and No/100 Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per year for the
                     first building and the equipment therein; and

                  b. Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($750,000.00)
                     per year for the second building and the equipment therein; and




                                              05/12/2020 Council Work Session Agenda-Page 42

[[page 43]]
                   c. Seven Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($700,000.00) per
                      year for the third building and the equipment therein; and

                   d. Six Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($650,000.00) per
                      year for the fourth building and the equipment therein; and

                   e. Six Hundred Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($600,000.00) per year
                      for the fifth building and the equipment therein; and

                   f. Five Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($550,000.00)
                      per year for the sixth building and the equipment therein and all
                      buildings and equipment thereafter.

        In addition to the foregoing, upon the expiration of twenty (20) years following the
completion of the first building, the Company shall make a payment in lieu of tax equal to
the ad valorem taxes that would have been payable to each of the City and the County on
the land only.

           3. The foregoing payments in lieu of tax are subject to increase as follows:

                   a. as to each building completed and all equipment located therein
                      from time to time, the payments in lieu of taxes relating thereto
                      provided for in the foregoing provisions of Section 2 shall increase
                      by five percent (5%) on each and every five (5)-year anniversary of
                      the commencement of the first (1st) full tax year (i.e., January 1)
                      following the date of completion of such building.

                   b. the amount of the payments in lieu of taxes provided for in the
                      foregoing provisions of Section 2 shall increase (i) by 20% for
                      buildings completed after the date which is ten (10) years following
                      the date of completion of the first building, but on or before the date
                      which is thirteen (13) years following the date of completion of the
                      first building, (ii) by 40% for buildings completed after the date
                      which is thirteen (13) years following the date of completion of the
                      first building, but on or before the date which is sixteen (16) years
                      following the date of completion of the first building, (iii) by 50%
                      for buildings completed after the date which is sixteen (16) years
                      following the date of completion of the first building, but on or
                      before the date which is twenty (20) years following the date of
                      completion of the first building, after the adjustment pursuant to
                      Subsections a. and c of this Section 3.

                   c. the payments in lieu of taxes provided for in the foregoing
                      provisions of Section2 shall increase (i) by 20% from and after the
                      date that is seven (7) years following the date of the PILOT lease
                      (the “Investment Date”) if the company has not made capital
                      expenditures of at least $500,000,000 or greater, but less than
                      $750,000,000, by the Investment Date, (ii) by 40% if the company

                                               05/12/2020 Council Work Session Agenda-Page 43

[[page 44]]
                      has not made capital expenditures of at least $250,000,000 or
                      greater, but less than $500,000,000, by the Investment Date, and (iii)
                      there shall be no further abatement if the company has not made
                      capital expenditures of at least $250,000,000 by the Investment
                      Date.

       4.     There shall be no tax abatement for buildings and the equipment located
              therein completed after the date which is twenty (20) years following the
              date of completion of the first building.

         WHEREAS, the City of Gallatin supports and desires to approve the PILOT
Program incentive proposed by the Industrial Development Board for Project Woolhawk and
desires to delegate authority to the Industrial Development Board to grant such payment in
lieu of tax incentives to the Company, in order to implement the purposes of the Act.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of the City of
Gallatin as follows:

       1.     That, pursuant to T.C.A. Section 7-53-305(b), the City Council of the City,
              hereby finds that the delegation of authority to the Industrial Development
              Board to grant payment in lieu of tax incentives for Project Woolhawk to
              the Company pursuant to the PILOT Program and the terms and conditions
              set forth in the Act and the granting of such incentives by the Industrial
              Development Board as payments in lieu of taxes or waiver of payments of
              taxes are deemed to be in furtherance of the Industrial Development Board’s
              public purposes as defined in the Act; and

       2.     The City Council of the City of Gallatin, Tennessee, hereby delegates
              authority to the Industrial Development Board to grant payment in lieu of
              tax incentives for Project Woolhawk to the Company pursuant to the PILOT
              Program and the terms and conditions set forth in the Act, and to negotiate,
              document and accept payments in lieu of taxes in connection therewith.

       3.     The Mayor and all officers and departments and agencies of the City of
              Gallatin, Tennessee, are hereby authorized and directed to take such further
              actions as are deemed necessary or advisable to carry out the intent and
              purposes of these Resolutions.


       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this resolution shall take effect upon passage,
