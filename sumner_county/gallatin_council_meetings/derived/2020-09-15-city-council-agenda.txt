Purpose of Report
As required by T.C.A. 6-51-108 (c), the Gallatin City Council shall conduct a public hearing and
present a report on the status or progress of plans of service (see Exhibit A).

Currently, the City comprises 33.52 +/- square miles and contains an estimated 44,tO7 personsi.
The listing of the Annexations and Plans of Service approved between March 2008 and
September 2020, including separate 31 actions annexing 4644 acres (7,25! square miles),
listed in Table 1 and identified in Exhibit 'B'. The table describes specific annexation
information and the status of provisions of Municipal Services.

The City prepared this report on the progress of Plans of Service for certain properties annexed
into the corporate limits of the City of Gallatin from March 18, 2008 through September 15,
2020, including properties slated for annexation on Spetember 15,2O2O. This includes
properties and rights-of-way annexed by Ordinance (prior to May 2015) and properties and
rights-of-way annexed by Resolution (after May 2015). Annexations completed prior to a 2015
change in Tennessee Statutes included voluntary and involuntary annexations approved by
Ordinances of the City Council. Annexations completed after 2015 included only voluntary
annexations approved by a Resolution of the City Council. All annexations require a separate
Resolution adopting a Plan of Service in conjunction with the annexation request.

Annexations for St. Blaise Estates and Long Hollow Pike areas (Ordinance Number }L2O2-I8 and
Ordinance Number o0842-L3l will continue to be addressed in subsequent updates of this report
until all identified improvements area installed or the Plans of Service are amended, modified
or until the necessary sewer infrastructure is installed to service both areas. Gallatin Public
Utilities prepared plans and retains the conveyance and treatment capacity to accommodate
properties to be served by the proposed utility line extensions but requires necessary rights-of-
way/easements from affected property owners to install two wastewater collection lines
servicing these areas. The remainder of the Plans of Service are satisfied or require the
developer to install the infrastructure.

The City prepares annexation reports which evaluate the fiscal impact of proposed annexations
on the provision of public services. Each annexation report is presented to the City Council.

DEPARTMENT: PLANNINGDEPARTMENT                                               AGENDA #

SUBJECT:
Resolution No. R2009-52 adopting an Interlocal Agreement with Sumner County addressing the
annexation of "The Meadows" and maintenance of a segment of the Old Highway 109 North, the
Computer Lane and the Cobbs Lane rights-of-way and the provision of emergency services.

SUMMARY:
An Interlocal Agreement is required by Tenn. Code Annotated, Title 6, Chapter 6, $6-51-104 (d)
between the City and the County for the Annexation and Plan of Service for the non-contiguous
 "The Meadows" property. The Interlocal Agreement allows the City the right the city to annex "The
Meadows" property and obligates the City to maintain a 4,200 foot long segment of the Old
Highw'ay 109 North right-of-way and the Computer Lane and Cobbs Lane rights-of-way and to
provide emergency services within the rights-of-way as a condition of annexing "The Meadows"
property. The Meadows property is not contiguous to the existing City limits but is located partly
within the Gallatin Planning Region and within the Urban Growth Boundary. The Planning
Commission recommended approval of "The Meadows" annexation and a Plan of Services as
required by Tenn. Code Annotated, Title 6, Chapter 6, $6-51-102(bX4); however, the Planning
Commission did not consider the lnterlocal Agreement. The Council approved Resolution No.
R1908-45 annexing the property and Resolution No. R1908-44 adopting a Plan of Service on
November 19,2019. The County Commision approved the Interlocal Agreement by adopting
County Resolution No. 2008-03 on August 17,2020.
