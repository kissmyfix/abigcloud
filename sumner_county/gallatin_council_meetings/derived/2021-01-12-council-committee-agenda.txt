 DEPARTMENT:          PLANNING DEPARTMENT                                      AGENDA# 7

SUBJECT:
Resolution No. R2101-03 recommending approval of an amendment to the Gallatin Urban Growth
Boundary to the County Coordinating Committee

 SUMMARY:
 The Planning Department requests City Council approve a resoultion recommending an amendment
 to the Gallatin Urban Growth Boundary. The Urban Growth Boundary was established in 2000 as
required by Tennessee Code Annotated, Title 6, Chapter 58, Sections 101-108. The Urban Growth
 Boundary was initially established to encompass an area that the City believed would be necessary
to accommodate existing and future urban development for a twenty year timeframe (until 2020).
The current Urban Growth Boundary encompaseses approximately 81 square miles including all
33.5 square miles in the city limits. At the request of Council staff made changes to the UGB and
prepared a report as required by statute. The Executive Summary of the report was presented to the
Council Committee at the December 8, 2020 meeting. The final report will be completed prior to
consideration of the Resolution on January 19th. If approved the Resolution/amendment will be sent
to the County Coordinating Committee. Other communities in umner County have also proposed
changes to their growth boundaries.
