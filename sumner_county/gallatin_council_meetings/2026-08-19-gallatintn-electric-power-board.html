Electric Power Board

An electric power board is hereby constituted and established for the purpose of taking and having supervision and control of the improvement, financing, operation, and maintenance of the City's electric system.

Membership Details

  • 3 members: One City Council appointee limited to term in office
  • Compensation permitted
