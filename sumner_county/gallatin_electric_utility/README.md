# sumner_county/gallatin_electric_utility/

## Purpose
Rate and service policy documentation for the Gallatin Department of Electricity (GDE), the municipal utility that serves the Meta campus.

## Contents
Rate sheets and service policy documents from January 2016 to present.

## Source Type
**Primary Source**

Official municipal utility documentation. GDE operates under TVA's wholesale rate structure; these documents reflect what was charged and to whom at any given point in time.

## Handling Instructions
- Cite by document date and rate schedule designation
- The 1.5% industrial rate Meta receives is part of the documented subsidy stack — cross-reference against standard residential and commercial rates in the same period to establish the spread
- Date range spans 2016 to present, predating the Woolhawk PILOT by five years — useful for establishing the baseline rate environment before Meta's arrival
- Rate changes over time are potentially significant; note when a rate tier appears, changes, or was created

## Notes
The 2016 start date is meaningful context: it shows what the rate structure looked like before Meta was in the picture. Any rate tier that emerged after 2018 (when Woolhawk negotiations began) warrants scrutiny.
