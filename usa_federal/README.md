# usa_federal/

## Purpose
Federal-level records — the IRS filings and federal standards that determine whether the Gallatin IDB's tax-exempt posture actually holds up.

## Contents
- `irs_990_data/` — Gallatin IDB 990 filings, Gibson County IDB comparison filings, IRS bulk-extract supporting data
- `tva/` — TVA material (FOIA reports; in-lieu-of-tax context)

## Source Type
**Primary Source** — federal filings and agency documents.

## Handling Instructions
The federal framework, condensed (this is now the canonical summary):

- **IRC § 115** — the actual federal basis for an IDB's exemption: income from an essential governmental function. This is separate from 501(c) status. An IDB filing a 990 as a **501(c)(4)** — as the Gallatin IDB does, alone among the eight TN IDBs that file at all — signals it has stepped outside (or lost) its § 115 governmental-function basis.
- **IRS Rev. Proc. 95-48** — the standard for "government instrumentality" status: governmental control/oversight, mandatory audits, funds used for public purposes. The IDB's own filings document failures against each factor (no mandatory audit, no oversight of expenditures, an administrator with no active business filing everything for free). The state constitutional property-tax exemption ultimately depends on this status.

## Notes
The chain to keep in view: state exemption ← government-instrumentality status ← Rev. Proc. 95-48 factors ← what the 990s themselves disclose. The IDB's federal paperwork is the evidence against its state-law posture.
