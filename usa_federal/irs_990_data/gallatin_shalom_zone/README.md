# usa_federal/irs_990_data/gallatin_shalom_zone/

## Purpose
Federal 990 filings by Gallatin Shalom Zone Inc., EIN 62-1800512. Held because the Gallatin
IDB's 990 preparer also prepares these — the same signature on a second Gallatin nonprofit's
federal filings, which is what makes them a comparison rather than a side interest.

## Contents
- `shalom2023.pdf` — as-filed IRS graphic print, tax year beginning 07-01-2023
- `shalom2024.pdf` — as-filed IRS graphic print, tax year beginning 07-01-2024
- `shalom2024-propublica.pdf` — ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer's render of the same 2024
  filing. Kept as a cross-source rendering, the way
  `state_of_tennessee/tn_property_assessments/sumner-assessment-bradford-confirmed.pdf` is.

`derived/` holds the page-anchored `.txt` extractions, the OCR'd intermediates the text was
read from, and `pdf-index.csv`. Both as-filed prints are scans, so their extractions carry
recognition errors.

## Source Type
**Primary Source** — federal tax filings signed under penalty of perjury.

## Handling Instructions
- Cite by filing year and part/line number, the same as the IDB filings.
- Both `shalom2023` and `shalom2024` are OCR'd scans. Confirm any figure against the page
  image before it appears in analysis or draft.
- Nothing here is an allegation about Shalom Zone. It is a second sample of the same
  preparer's work.

Created 2026-08-19. The four files sat loose in `irs_990_data/` under their download names —
