# web_articles/

## Purpose
Copy-pasted articles gathered from online sources during the investigation — used to establish what the public record contains and what it doesn't.

## Contents
Text from news articles, trade publications, press releases, and other online sources, plus scanned pages from newspaper archives. Mix of publication types and dates.

**Every file carries a provenance header** — `SOURCE`, `HEADLINE`, `BYLINE`, `DATE`, `URL`, `ARCHIVE`, `RETRIEVED`, `WHY SAVED` — then `---`, then the text. Where a field is genuinely unknown it says so rather than being omitted. Normalised across every file 2026-08-19.

These are scans,

## Source Type
**Secondary Source**

Published journalism and online reporting. Reflects other reporters' and publications' framing and may contain their own analytical conclusions alongside facts.

## Handling Instructions
- **Holding pen — do not read or index this directory unless directed to a specific article.** Much of what lands here is parked in the moment of discovery and hasn't been absorbed into the investigation yet
- Note original publication and date when citing
- Do not reproduce at length — copyright applies to all published text; paraphrase and attribute
- Useful for establishing what has and hasn't been covered — the absence of coverage on specific topics (the IDB mechanism, the PILOT structure) is itself a documented finding
- Treat data center trade publication coverage with appropriate skepticism — industry trade press often reflects PR framing
- The Fenton on-record quote ("will become the largest revenue source in the county") came from a Data Center Knowledge interview; if that article is here, treat it as a sourced primary quote
