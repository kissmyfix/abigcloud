# tn_sos_filings/

## Purpose
Tennessee Secretary of State corporate records for entities and individuals in scope — the
filings that establish when a corporation existed, who its registered agent was, and when
that changed. Filed here by source (the Secretary of State), not by who they are about.

## Contents
- `2002-preston-stark-inc-sos-business-detail.pdf` — SOS business detail for PRESTON STARK,
  INC., control no. 000438125. For-profit corporation, formed in Tennessee, initial filing
  12/12/2002, status *Inactive — Terminated*, AR due date 04/01/2010, 1,000 shares,
  principal office 1100 Hatcher Lane, Columbia TN. Recovered 2026-07-29 from a superseded
  project copy where it sat under a mangled filename; a shorter download of the same record
  was discarded as a subset.

## Source Type
**Primary Source** — records filed with and published by the Tennessee Secretary of State.

## Handling Instructions
- Citable as primary evidence of corporate existence, dates, officers, and registered-agent
  history. Cite the SOS record and its control number.
- **A corporation sharing a person's name is not evidence about that person's public role.**
  PRESTON STARK, INC. is a terminated Columbia TN for-profit; Preston Stark's significance
  to this investigation is as Gallatin IDB director, registered agent, and 990 filer. Those
  are separate facts and must not be blended into one claim.
- The Gallatin IDB's own SOS corporate history lives with the board's records at
  `sumner_county/gallatin_idb_data/1994-2026-idb-gallatin.pdf`, not here — it predates this
  directory and stays where sessions already look for it.
