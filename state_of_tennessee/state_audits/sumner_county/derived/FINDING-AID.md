# Finding Aid — state_audits/sumner_county/

**What this is.** A content map of the six documents in the parent directory: what each
one actually contains, where in it to look, and which questions it can and cannot answer.
`pdf-index.csv` next to this file tells you whether a document is *trustworthy*. This file
tells you what is *in* it. Those are different questions and you usually arrive with the
second one.

Built 2026-08-24. Page numbers are citable printed pages unless a section says otherwise.

---

## Read this before using this directory

**Five of the six documents are not county audits.** They are the Sumner County Board of
Education's *Internal School Funds* annual reports: the activity accounts of individual
schools. Beech High School's balance sheet, athletic funds, club accounts, fundraiser
receipts, cafeteria collections.

That does not make them irrelevant. **They are where Meta's grants to individual schools
are itemised**, year by year and school by school, which is the best evidence held here on
the corporate-giving question. See *Series 2* below.

What they do not contain is any mention of the Industrial Development Board, PILOT
payments, payments in lieu of taxes, or industrial development, in any year. Verified by
full-text search across all five, 2026-08-24:

| Document | "IDB" / Industrial Development Board | "PILOT" | "payment in lieu" |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2020 internal school funds | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| FY2022 internal school funds | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| FY2023 internal school funds | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| FY2024 internal school funds | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| FY2025 internal school funds | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **FY2019 county annual financial report** | **4** | 0 | 0 |

The `2559-*-sumcoact-*` filename pattern is the tell. `2559` is the Comptroller's entity
number and `sumcoact` is *Sumner County activity funds*, not the county's general audit.

**Consequence.** The reconciliation the parent README proposes — checking whether the
Gallatin IDB's reported FY2025 distributions of $2,291,692 to Sumner County and $640,457
to Sumner County Schools appear on the receiving end — **cannot be performed with these
documents.** School activity funds are a separate reporting universe from county revenue.
The correct source is the county's annual financial report or the school department's
audited financial statements, and only FY2019 of the former is held here.

## What is missing

- **County annual financial reports FY2020 through FY2025.** Only FY2019 is held. The
  parent README records FY2021 as the sole gap; the real gap is six consecutive years of
  the document type the directory is named for.
- **Sumner County School Department audited financial statements**, any year. The
  discretely presented component unit, which is where a statutory PILOT share to schools
  would land, is summarized inside the county report and not held separately.

### Items of investigative interest

**p. 58 — Related Organizations note.** The county's own audited statements describe its
relationship to the boards it appoints:

> Related Organizations – The Industrial Development Board of Sumner County, the Health
> Board of Sumner County, the Health and Educational Facilities Board of Sumner County,
> and the Health and Safety Standards Board of Sumner County are related organizations of
> Sumner County. The Sumner County Commission's Committee on Committees nominates and the
> Sumner County Commission confirms the board members, but the county's accountability for
> these organizations does not extend beyond making the appointments.

This is the accountability boundary stated by the county, in an audited document, rather
than inferred. Note the scope: it disclaims accountability beyond appointment, which is
narrower than disclaiming any relationship at all. Note also that this is the *county* IDB,
a separate entity from the Gallatin city IDB.

**p. 85 — Hendersonville IDB tax increment financing.** Under *Pledges of Receivables and
Future Revenues*:

- The Sumner County Commission and the City of Hendersonville permitted the Industrial
  Development Board of the City of Hendersonville to pledge all TIF revenues from the
  Indian Lake Village Economic Development Area, for up to 30 years.
- Pledged against $15,000,000 of adjustable rate tax-exempt increment bonds (Series 2007A)
  and $3,000,000 of adjustable rate taxable increment bonds (Series 2007B).
- Issued 29 March 2007, final maturities 1 May 2036.
- Project: a four-lane divided median east/west connector road, among other works.
- Sumner County is not liable for the note. Its only obligation is to collect and remit
  TIF revenue to the IDB.
- Records held at the Industrial Development Board of the City of Hendersonville,
  101 Maple Drive North, Hendersonville.

Directly relevant to the Hendersonville topic page, which currently has no archive behind
it. A 30-year pledge issued in 2007 is still running.

**p. 47 — Industrial Development appropriation.** County budget line under *Other
Operations*: $100,000 appropriated, $100,000 expended, no variance. Detail at **p. 204**
shows the full $100,000 is a single "Contributions" line, with no personnel or operating
breakdown. Recipient is not named in the audit.

For comparison on the same page: Tourism $650,000, Contributions to Other Agencies
$271,654, Veterans' Services $92,758.

### Audit findings, FY2019

One finding only.

**2019-001 — Information Technology Equipment is Missing.** Internal control significant
deficiency under Government Auditing Standards. Director of Schools. Not a repeat finding.
Corrective action plan at p. 242, prepared by Andy Brown (Director of Maintenance), Chris
Brown (Director of IT) and Amanda Prichard (CFO); anticipated completion July 2019.
Signed by Del R. Phillips III, Director of Schools.

Nothing in the FY2019 findings touches the IDB, PILOT agreements, or economic development.

**Best Practice, p. 243.** The Division of Local Government Audit recommends Sumner County
adopt a central system of accounting, budgeting, and purchasing covering all county
departments. Standard comptroller language, issued to many Tennessee counties; note it as
context for how decentralized county financial control was, not as a finding.

## Series 2 — Board of Education Internal School Funds, FY2020 and FY2022–FY2025

`2559-2020-s-sumcoact-rpt-cpa811-12-24-20.pdf` (167 pp)
`2559-2022-s-sumcoact-rpt-cpa522-12-21-22.pdf` (226 pp)
`2559-2023-s-sumcoact-rpt-cpa522-12-21-23.pdf` (226 pp)
`2559-2024-s-sumcoact-rpt-cpa522-12-24-24.pdf` (230 pp)
`2559-2025-s-sumcoact-rpt-cpa522-12-27-25.pdf` (212 pp)

All born-digital, extraction authoritative. FY2021 not held.

**Scope.** Regulatory-basis financial statements for the activity funds of individual
Sumner County schools. Each report carries a combined balance sheet and combined statement
of revenues and expenditures across all schools, then repeats both statements school by
school. Beech High School, Gallatin High School, Station Camp, and so on.

**Arrangement, consistent across years.** Introductory Section (table of contents, then a
Directory of School Officials) · Financial Section (Independent Auditor's Report, then
combined statements, then individual school statements alphabetically).

**The Directory of School Officials** is the one part of these reports with standing
research value for placing a person in a role on a date: named officials and board members
by year, five years of it. FY2024 has it at p. 9.

### Meta grants, school by school

**This is the reason these five documents matter.** They are the receiving end of Meta's
corporate giving programme, itemised per school, per year, with balances carried forward.
Directly relevant to the open question in `memory/citation-worklist.md` about whether
Meta's "70+ grants and sponsorships provided locally since 2021" is the same money as the
IDB's school payments or a separate stream.

Grants appear under three labels: `Meta Data Center Comm Grant`, `META Community Action
Grant`, and plain `Meta Grant`.

**Do not total these from this table.** The column headers on the underlying statements
have not been verified line by line, and the receipts-versus-balance columns are easy to
misread in `-layout` output. Open the page, read the statement header, then compute.

**Search trap.** Grepping these reports for `meta` returns `Metal/CAD Grant` in every year,
which is a metalworking and drafting programme at the high schools and has nothing to do
with Meta. It is the larger of the two by value. Match on `Meta Grant`, `Meta Data Center`
or `META Community` instead.

**What these cannot tell you.** Anything about county revenue, PILOT receipts, tax
abatement, industrial development, or the IDB by name. Verified by full-text search: zero
hits in all five years. Meta money reaches these documents as ordinary grant income to an
individual school's activity fund, with no reference to the agreement that produced it.

## How to search this directory

Grep the `.txt` extractions in this directory, never the PDFs. Then open only the pages
that matter, and cite the printed page in the PDF.

```
grep -n -i "search term" *.txt
```

`[[page N]]` markers give the PDF page index. In the FY2019 county report those match the
printed page numbers exactly; that alignment was verified, and should not be assumed for
the school-funds reports.
