# Finding Aid — state_audits/gallatin_city/

Eleven consecutive City of Gallatin annual financial reports, FY2015 through FY2025, plus
three excerpt PDFs. `pdf-index.csv` beside this file says whether each extraction is
trustworthy. This file says what is in them and where.

Built 2026-08-24 by full-text search across all eleven years. Page numbers are `[[page N]]`
PDF indices; **verify against the printed page before citing** — alignment was confirmed
for the Sumner FY2019 report and has not been checked here.

---

## Why this directory matters

This is the City's own audited account of its relationship to the Industrial Development
Board, told the same way for eleven years running, which makes changes in the telling
visible. Nothing in this directory is currently cited by any page.

### The fund's stated purpose, FY2015–FY2023

Consistent wording, FY2015–FY2020, at pp. 74, 74, 74, 75, 77, 77:

> The Industrial Development Board is used to account for economic development activity in
> the City.

Reworded from FY2021 (p. 83) and carried through FY2023 (p. 96):

> Industrial Development Board – To account for activities by the City to encourage
> economic development.

### FY2023, p. 47 — Related Organizations note

The City's fullest statement of the relationship, in the notes on the reporting entity:

> ...Housing Authority) and the Industrial Development Board of Gallatin, Tennessee (the
> IDB), but the City's accountability for these organizations does not extend beyond making
> the appointments. Board members of the Housing Authority and the IDB are appointed by the
> Mayor, but the City does not provide funding, has no obligation for the debt issued by
> the Housing Authority and the IDB, and cannot impose its will upon the operations of the
> Housing Authority and the IDB. Accordingly, the Housing Authority have not been included
> in the reporting entity.

Three things to read carefully rather than assume:

1. **"the City does not provide funding."** Present tense, FY2023. The FY2015–FY2019
   statements above record general-fund transfers into a fund named Industrial Development
   Board. Whether those are the same thing turns on whether the City *fund* and the IDB
   *entity* are distinct, which this note does not settle and which belongs with
   `memory/dual-status-contradiction.md`.
2. **The final sentence names only the Housing Authority.** As printed, it does not say
   the IDB is excluded from the reporting entity, though the paragraph's structure implies
   it. Read the surrounding pages before characterising it.
3. Compare against the same note in the Sumner County FY2019 report (p. 58), which uses
   near-identical language for the *county* IDB. The wording is a comptroller template, so
   its presence is not itself a finding; departures from it would be.

### FY2020, p. 7 — the announcement, in the City's own audit

In the management discussion and analysis:

> Facebook announced that they are building an $800 million data center in Gallatin.

The only mention of Facebook or Meta in any of the eleven years. Full-text search returns
zero hits for "Meta" and zero for "PILOT" across the entire directory, all years.

### Joint venture, FY2023 p. 47

Immediately following the related-organizations note: the City is a participant in the
**Sumner County Resource Authority**, a joint venture of Sumner County and the Cities of
Gallatin and Hendersonville operating a solid waste energy recovery plant. The City holds
no equity interest. Complete statements available from the City Finance Director. Not
otherwise represented in this archive.

obvious trap in this directory.
