## Notes
**FY2025 figures, arithmetic-verified:** PILOT revenue $74,829; total public support $80,561. Expenses $62,603, of which **$48,000 is a single line reading "Civitas"** — 77% of everything the board spent. Change in net assets +$17,958; net assets $93,997 → $111,955, including a $10,544 certificate of deposit.

**It distributed none of its PILOT revenue to the county or schools** — unlike the IDB, which passed $2.93M through.

**Civitas Agency is a Nashville marketing firm** that produced the "Gallatin Gets It" campaign and website for the Gallatin Economic Development Agency (*Trade and Industry Development*, 2023-01-17). **Not verified:** that this $48,000 funded that campaign, or any legal relationship between the HHFB and GEDA. The audit gives a proper noun and a number and nothing else.

**Board of Directors, June 30, 2025** — identical to the IDB's, same order: Neil Burgess (Chair), Len Assante (Treasurer), Joanne Walker (Secretary), Stan Carver, Jessie Maness, Allen Ramsey, Phil Carver, Derrick Jackson, Don Cunningham.

Note 1 describes the board as "responsible for recruitment and facilitate development in the City of Gallatin" — economic development language in a board chartered for health, educational, and housing facilities.
