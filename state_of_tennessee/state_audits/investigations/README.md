# state_audits/investigations/

## Purpose
Comptroller investigative reports and Sumner County grand jury presentments, kept for a **narrative contrast angle** — not as a thread of their own.

Brandon's reason for saving them, in his words: *"they're all minor investigations surrounding the state or city prosecuting individual employees and citizens over rather minor infractions compared to the scales my current investigation centers... so they'll attack citizen A over here while keeping their blinders firmly over their eyes when money works to their own gain."*

## Contents
- `2026-03-30-comptroller-investigative-report-city-of-gallatin.pdf` — Comptroller's Investigative Report, City of Gallatin, March 30, 2026. Jason E. Mumpower, Division of Investigations; addressed to Mayor Paige Brown, copies to the Governor and Attorney General. 5 pages, born-digital.
- `2026-03-grand-jury-presentment-state-v-smith.pdf` — grand jury presentment, Sumner County Criminal Court, March Term 2026, *State v. James Roger Smith Jr.*, alleging conduct from July 31, 2019 to November 18, 2025 depriving the state of lawful revenue. 4 pages, image-only; OCR'd copy in `derived/`.
- `2017-04-grand-jury-presentment-state-v-morris.pdf` — grand jury presentment, April Term 2017, *State v. Jennifer Pappas Morris*, theft from the City of Gallatin City Recorder's Office, June–November 2016. 3 pages, image-only; OCR'd copy in `derived/`.
- `derived/` — OCR'd copies, page-anchored text, `pdf-index.csv`.

## Source Type
**Primary Source** — court records and a Comptroller investigative report.

## Handling Instructions
- **Weight these correctly.** They are small-dollar enforcement actions against individuals. Their value is rhetorical contrast with the scale of the PILOT arrangements, not equivalence. Do not let them expand into a parallel investigation.
- These name **private individuals accused or convicted of crimes**. Anything published about them must be sourced to the document itself and stated with the precision the record supports — charged, presented, pled, convicted are different words.
- Two of the three have **OCR text layers only**. Never quote from the extraction; quote from the page image.

## Notes
The 2017 and 2026 presentments bracket a nine-year window of city-government prosecutions, which is the span the contrast argument would draw on.
