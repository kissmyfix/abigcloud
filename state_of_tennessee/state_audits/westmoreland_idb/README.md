## Notes
**Audited under GASB as "a component unit of the City of Westmoreland, Tennessee."** Note 1 calls the Board "an agency for these governmental entities," appointed by the City of Westmoreland and Sumner County. Includes government-wide and fund financial statements, Management's Discussion and Analysis, GASB 14 reporting-entity and GASB 54 fund-balance notes, deferred outflows/inflows, a full page on state-statute deposit collateralization at 105%, and a long-term debt schedule — **none of which appear in either Gallatin board's audit.**

**FY2025:** net position $752,317 (up $179,779); revenues $286,039 from rental income and city donations, not PILOT; paid off a $213,181 building loan.

**Material weakness 2025-001, separation of duties — repeated since 2011-001**, with a management response and a Corrective Action Plan on city letterhead. Fourteen consecutive years. The Gallatin boards, with no employees and far more money moving, have no Schedule of Findings section at all.
