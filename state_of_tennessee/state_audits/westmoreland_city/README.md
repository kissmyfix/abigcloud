# state_audits/westmoreland_city/

## Purpose
Annual audits of the City of Westmoreland, the municipal half of the Westmoreland control
pair. Its Industrial Development Board is audited separately in `../westmoreland_idb/`, and
the point of holding both is to see how a small Sumner County city and its IDB report
against each other.

## Contents
- `1901-2024-c-westmoreland-rpt-cpa517-12-21-24.pdf` — FY2024, filed December 21, 2024.
- `1901-2025-c-westmoreland-rpt-cpa517-1-27-26.pdf` — FY2025, filed January 27, 2026.
- `derived/` — page-anchored text and `pdf-index.csv`.

`1901` is the Comptroller's entity number for the city.

## Source Type
**Primary Source.** Comptroller-filed annual audits.

## Handling Instructions
- Audited under **GASB**, like a municipality: the reports carry Management's Discussion and
  Analysis, governmental and business-type activities, and **discretely presented component
  units**. That last phrase is the reason this directory matters. Compare it against the
  City of Gallatin's treatment of its own boards.
- Verify figures against the page image before publishing them.
- Cite printed page numbers, not `[[page N]]` markers.
