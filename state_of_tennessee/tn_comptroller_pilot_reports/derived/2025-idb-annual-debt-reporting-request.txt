To:            Industrial Development Board Contact
From:          Sheila Reed, Director
               Division of Local Government Finance
Date:          September 23, 2025
Subject:       FY 2025 – Request for Annual IDB Information - Outstanding Debt and Contacts
               Tenn. Code Ann. § 7-53-304


Thank you for partnering with us as we gather important information for Tennessee’s industrial
development boards. Our online submission process enables us to prepopulate the information you
input last year into this year’s form. Please be sure to use the enclosed Steps to Annual IDB
Report Submission when you complete the form. Following is other helpful information.

Reporting Deadline
You have 90 calendar days from the close of your IDB’s fiscal year to complete the information;
however, we encourage earlier reporting when possible.

IDBs With No Debt
If your IDB has no outstanding debt, including no conduit debt, the annual reporting is still
required. Once you access the online form on our website you will need to update general
information about your IDB.

Required Notice for Defaults, Covenant Violations, and Credit Rating Downgrades
IDBs are required to file a notice of default on any of its debt obligations within fifteen (15) days
of the default. Any debt covenant violations or credit rating downgrades must be reported within
ten (10) days. Reporting requirements and forms that comply with requirements of the Tennessee
State Funding Board are available on our website at: tncot.cc/idb.

Questions
If you need assistance with your submission, you may contact Alex Lucas, Data Specialist, at
Alex.Lucas@cot.tn.gov.

  1. Access the online form on our website at tncot.cc/idb

  2. Click on the button labeled: Complete Annual IDB Report then:
          • Choose your entity name from the 1st drop-down menu.
          • Choose fiscal year end 2025 from the 2nd drop-down menu.
          • Check the confirm box; then click the continue button.




  3. Update changes to contact information:
         • board members;
         • authorized representative (the individual completing the form); and
