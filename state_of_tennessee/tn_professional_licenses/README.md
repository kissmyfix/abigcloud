# tn_professional_licenses

State licensing records for the professionals who sign the documents this
investigation runs on. Currently the two Hendersonville CPAs who between them
handle the audit and the federal filings of the Gallatin IDB.

## What is here

| `2026-08-18-tn-accountancy-license-young-johnny-p.png` | Johnny P Young, CPA, licence 4894 | Paid preparer of the Gallatin IDB's Forms 990 from FY2022 and of the Gallatin Shalom Zone's Forms 990 — both sides of a related-party flow. |

The other 161 reports were deleted as scope creep. |

`derived/` holds a field-for-field transcription of each screenshot with provenance headers,
page-anchored text for the PDF, and `pdf-index.csv`.

## Source and how to reproduce

Tennessee Department of Commerce and Insurance, **Verify Public Search**,
<https://verify.tn.gov> (redirects to `search.cloud.commerce.tn.gov`). Profession:
Accountancy. The tool sits behind a CAPTCHA and publishes no bulk dataset, so
these were captured by hand as browser screenshots on 2026-08-18. Records are
stated to update daily, so a later capture may differ; keep these rather than
overwrite them.

Detail pages carry stable-looking record ids:

- Poole: `search.cloud.commerce.tn.gov/search/C596759/detail`
- Young: `search.cloud.commerce.tn.gov/search/C587756/detail`

## Handling

**The disciplinary alert says only that discipline occurred.** It gives no date,
no cause, and no indication whether it touched audit or governmental work.
Nothing here supports any characterisation beyond the words on the record. The
underlying order would be in Tennessee State Board of Accountancy meeting
minutes or NASBA's enforcement records, neither of which is in this archive yet.

Neither record shows a firm affiliation connecting the two men, and their licence
numbers and issue dates are fourteen years apart. **There is no documented
professional relationship between them in these records** — only that two sole
practitioners in the same small city hold the audit and the tax work of the same
cluster of entities.
