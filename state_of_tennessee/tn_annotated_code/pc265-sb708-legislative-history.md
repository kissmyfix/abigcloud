# Bill Summary

Under present law provisions governing classification and assessment of property for property tax purposes, all mineral interests and all other interests of whatever character, not defined as products of the soil, in real property, including the interest that the lessee may have in and to the improvements erected upon land where the fee, reversion, or remainder therein is exempt to the owner, and which interest or interests is or are owned separately from the general freehold, are assessed to the owner thereof, separately from the other interests in such real estate, which other interests are assessed to the owner thereof, all of which is assessed as real property.

This bill adds that all interests in real property, including a leasehold interest, with respect to which there is a lawful agreement between the lessee and a governmental entity, including, but not limited to, an industrial development board, regional megasite authority, housing authority, or a health, educational and housing facilities board, must be assessed to the governmental entity that is the owner of the fee interest in the property, and is subject to the present law provisions governing: the tax exemption for industrial development corporations (exempting such a corporation and all properties at any time owned by it, and the income and revenues from the properties, and all bonds issued by it, and the income from the bonds, from all state taxation); the tax exemption under the Regional Megasite Authority Act (exempting the authority and all properties at any time owned by the authority, the income and revenues from the properties and all bonds issued by the authority and the income from the bonds from all state taxation in the state); the tax exemption for special purpose corporations and associations (exempting the corporation and all properties at any time owned by it and the income and revenues therefrom and all bonds issued by it and the income therefrom from all state taxation); the tax exemption for all property of the United States, the state of Tennessee, any county, or any incorporated town, city or taxing district in the state that is used exclusively for public, county or municipal purposes; or the tax exemption for government bonds and notes. This bill specifies that these provisions apply so long as the agreement provides that following any period of abatement of all or a portion of the ad valorem taxes, the lessee must make payments in lieu of taxes for the remainder of the term of the lease in an amount equal to the ad valorem taxes that, but for ownership of a project by such a governmental entity, would have been due and payable pursuant to present law.

ON APRIL 1, 2019, THE SENATE ADOPTED AMENDMENT #1 AND PASSED SENATE BILL 708, AS AMENDED.

AMENDMENT #1 rewrites this bill to provide that the present law provisions described above in the first paragraph of the bill summary apply unless the lessee's or a sublessee's interest in any such real property, including any improvements erected upon the land, is the subject of a lawful agreement between a lessee and a local government, or instrumentality thereof, for payments in lieu of taxes, entered into or amended on or after the effective date of this bill as amended, in which case such property will be assessed solely to such governmental entity and will be subject to all applicable exemptions.

Also, present law provides that:

(1) Leased personal property used by a public utility company or modern market telecommunications provider must be assessed to such company or provider, unless such property is the subject of a lawful agreement between the lessee and a local government for payments in lieu of taxes; and
(2) Other leased personal property must be classified according to the lessee's use and assessed to the lessee, unless such property is the subject of a lawful agreement between the lessee and a local government for payments in lieu of taxes.

This amendment revises item (2) above to make it also applicable to agreements between the lessee and an instrumentality of the local government for payment in lieu of taxes.
