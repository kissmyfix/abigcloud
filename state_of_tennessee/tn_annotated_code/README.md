# state_of_tennessee/tn_annotated_code/

## Purpose

## Handling Instructions
- Cite statute by T.C.A. section and subsection. Cite the 2019 act as **Public Chapter 265 (2019)** when quoting enacted language, not by bill number.
- **The as-introduced and as-enacted files are the same legislation before and after amendment, and their operative text differs.** Quote the stage you mean.
- `pc265-sb708-as-enacted.pdf` is an **OCR'd scan** — the extraction contains recognition errors (`laMul` for `lawful`, mangled signature block). Quote enacted language from the page image, not the `.txt`.
- When a claim in the project rests on Leon's or Isbell's characterization of a statute, upgrade the citation to the text here whenever the section is available.

## Notes

**"Instrumentality" was added by amendment, not introduced with the bill.** Verified 2026-07-28: the as-introduced bill contains **zero** occurrences; the enacted chapter contains **two** — § 67-5-502(c) and (d) both rewritten to read "between the lessee and a local government, **or instrumentality thereof**, for payments in lieu of taxes."

**The amendment also removed the price floor.** Per the fiscal memo, amendment 006478 "removes the requirement that payment in lieu of tax (PILOT) agreements are in an amount equal to the ad valorem taxes due," and limits the act to agreements entered into or amended **on or after July 1, 2019**. The introduced version had required PILOT "in an amount equal to the ad valorem taxes."

**The fiscal estimate was rewritten alongside it.** Original bill: "A recurring shift in local government revenue to state and local governmental entities **exceeding $2,720,400**." As amended: "the extent and timing of which is **unknown**. Any net impact to local revenue is considered **not significant**."

**Amendment #1 was adopted by the Senate on April 1, 2019**, per the legislative history — "ON APRIL 1, 2019, THE SENATE ADOPTED AMENDMENT #1 AND PASSED SENATE BILL 708, AS AMENDED." The fiscal memorandum identifies it as amendment 006478.

**It passed unanimously, and every committee vote was a voice vote.** House floor 96–0 (2 present not voting), Senate floor 33–0, Senate State & Local Government Committee 9–0. All five House committee and subcommittee actions are recorded only as "Voice Vote - Ayes Prevail" — no roll call, no recorded opposition anywhere in the record.

**Effective-date discrepancy — unresolved.** The enacted text limits application to agreements "entered into or amended on or after **the effective date of this section**," and Section 3 provides the act "shall take effect upon becoming a law" (passed April 18, 2019). The fiscal memorandum instead assumes twice that it "only applies to PILOT agreements entered into or amended on or after **July 1, 2019**." The bill summary says "the effective date of this bill as amended." These are not the same date, and the fiscal memo's "not significant" conclusion rests on its version.

Act took effect "upon becoming a law, the public welfare requiring it."
