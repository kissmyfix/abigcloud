⚑ The 2022 Part 3 "No" answer to the question "Did the organization make significant changes in how it conducts program services?" directly contradicts the mission statement change documented in the same filing's Part I and Part III. Both statements appear on the same form signed under penalty of perjury by Preston Stark on November 13, 2023.

The IDB is not listed in the IRS's Exempt Organizations Business Master File — it has never received formal IRS recognition of 501(c)(4) status. It is a self-declared exempt organization. No 990 filings exist prior to FY2020 despite documented Bradford Company and Beretta USA Corp. activity under IDB structure from at least 2013. The Woolhawk LLC lease (Meta Platforms) executed October 2, 2020 — within the FY2020 tax year — and Woolhawk is never named in any IDB filing.

Sumner County government PILOT column reads $0 in every year of the Tennessee State Comptroller's PILOT registry for Woolhawk LLC (2021–2025).
