# Neil Burgess

Chair of the Gallatin Industrial Development Board from tax year 2024, after three years on
the board. Listed at one hour a week and zero compensation every year he appears. Still
Chair on the FY2025 audit. Nothing else about him appears anywhere in this archive.

## Why he matters

- **He chairs the board for the year the money leaves.** The FY2024 filing lists him as
  CHAIR and reports $2,291,692 paid out as "PILOT fees distributed," more than the year's
  revenue, draining net assets from $904,342 to $148,027. **The recipients are not itemised
  anywhere in the filing.**
- **He is the sitting chair on the FY2025 audit**, dated 30 June 2025 —
  `state_of_tennessee/state_audits/gallatin_idb/`, officer list, alongside Len Assante as
  Treasurer.
- **He moved up through the chairs during the deal years.** Board member on the FY2021 and
  FY2022 filings, **Vice-Chair** on FY2023, Chair on FY2024. He does not appear on the FY2020
  990-EZ at all, so his first listed year is FY2021.
- **The board's own disclosure applies to him too.** Schedule O says the 990 is reviewed
  after filing, not before.

**What this does not establish.** Chairing the board in the year of a distribution is not
evidence of anything about that distribution. The missing itemisation is a fact about the
filing, not about him. There are no minutes and no agendas, so there is no record of what
he said, moved, or voted. Do not characterise him beyond the record.
