# Leonard Assante

Treasurer of the Gallatin Industrial Development Board, and Treasurer of the Gallatin
Health, Educational & Housing Facilities Board — the same nine people, two statutory
shells. Teaches at Volunteer State Community College, a Tennessee Board of Regents school
in Gallatin (per his own 2008 Tennessean byline).

## Why he matters

- **Treasurer of both boards.** He signs, or is responsible for, the financial position of
  two entities audited under two different frameworks by the same sole practitioner
  (John R. Poole) on the same day, July 31, 2025 — see `memory/PINBOARD.md` 2026-07-28.
- Listed on the FY2024 IDB Form 990 board roster (`memory/MEMORY.md`) and on the identical
  nine-person rosters captured from gallatintn.gov /509 and /503 on 2026-07-06.
- **Reappointed Jan/Feb 2021** to a six-year term, presented by Fenton — the same council
  packets that move another $127,769 from the Donations account to Building Codes
  (`memory/TIMELINE.md`, `sumner_county/gallatin_council_meetings/README.md`).
- **His IDB term expires July 31, 2026** — alongside Burgess and Stan Carver.
  Reappointments imminent; watch the council agenda.
