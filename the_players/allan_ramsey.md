# Allan Ramsey (Allen Ramsey)

Chair of the Gallatin Industrial Development Board across the four filings covering the
Woolhawk deal, tax years 2020 through 2023, then an ordinary board member from 2024. Listed
at two hours a week and zero compensation every year. Nothing else about him appears
anywhere in this archive.

## Why he matters

- **He held the chair for the whole Woolhawk window.** Chair on the FY2020 990-EZ, the
  FY2021, FY2022 and FY2023 filings. That covers the year Resolution R2005-24 delegated the
  PILOT terms, the years the board reported nothing to the county, and FY2023, when revenue
  reached $1,796,648 and net assets climbed to $904,342 rather than being distributed.
- **He stepped down from the chair for the year the money moved.** The FY2024 filing lists
  him as BOARD MEMBER at the same two hours, with Neil Burgess taking the chair. FY2024 is
  the year $2,291,692 goes out as "PILOT fees distributed" to recipients the filing never
  names.
- **The board's own disclosure applies to him.** Schedule O says the board reviews the Form
  990 after it is filed, not before. Whatever the chair's title implies about oversight of
  the filings, the filings say the review happened afterwards.
- **His given name is unstable in the record.** "Allen" on the FY2020 990-EZ, "Allan" from
  FY2021 onward. Worth carrying both spellings into any records request or search.

**What this does not establish.** Holding a title is not a finding about conduct. Nothing in
the archive shows what he knew, what he voted for, or what he was told, because the
board publishes no minutes and no agendas. He is unpaid and listed at two hours a week.
Do not characterise him beyond the record.
