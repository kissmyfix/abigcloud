# Preston Stark

The Gallatin IDB's registered agent since July 12, 2021, and its "Board Administrative
Manager" (pstarkconsulting@aol.com). Not a board member — the paid administrator who files
the paperwork. He took the registered-agent role in the Woolhawk signing window.

## Why he matters

- **2021-07-12: registered agent changes from Susan High-McAuley (City Attorney) to
  Stark.** The second of the two moves that walked the IDB out of city legal oversight —
  the first being the July 19, 2018 principal-office change from the City Attorney's office
  (Rm 212) to GEDA (Rm 210). High-McAuley remained registered agent for three years *after*
  the office moved; Stark's appointment closes that gap. See `memory/MEMORY.md` and
  `memory/dual-status-contradiction.md`.
- **2021-11-14: signs the IDB's first-ever Form 990** (FY Jul 2020–Jun 2021): $100,048
  revenue, an unexplained $102,948 in opening assets, and a formation year claimed as 2020
  for a board chartered in 1994.
- Listed as "Stark admin" on the FY2024 990 board roster.
- Named in `angles/rent-mechanism.md` as an untraced destination: of $42.18M moving through
  the tracked Sumner entities, 90.2% is rent to the IDB as landlord, and where it actually
  goes — IDB overhead, Stark's fees, or something else — has not been traced. Follow-up
  items 3 and 4 in that file are his.
- The `$1,000 lens` in `memory/MEMORY.md` applies to his ledgers specifically.
- **Treasurer of Gallatin Shalom Zone**, confirmed directly from its current FY2024–25 Form 990 (filed Nov 2025) — joined that board the same year (2020) he became the IDB's paid administrator and signed its first 990. Shalom Zone owns the former Union High School building at 600 Small St (deeded 2004) where a $1.16M+ "new daycare" is now being credited to a different nonprofit (Gallatin Day Care Center) operating as a tenant with no traceable state childcare license of its own. Full knot: `memory/MEMORY.md` (Shalom Zone/Gallatin Daycare entries) and `memory/TIMELINE.md` #shalom-zone / #gdc.
