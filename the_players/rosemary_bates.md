# Rosemary Bates

Director of the Gallatin Economic Development Agency (GEDA) — Interim Director in FY2024,
Director from FY2025 per the city's own ACFR officials list, following James Fenton. Also a
past board chair of Gallatin Shalom Zone, stepping down roughly a year before 2026-08.

## Why she matters

- **The FY2023 pivot's other half.** Fenton is listed as the city's Director of Economic
  Development in every ACFR FY2015–FY2023; Bates appears as Interim Director in FY2024, then
  Director in FY2025 — the same fiscal window the city's IDB fund closes and the corporation's
  own PILOT revenue (not run through city books) hits $2,210,996. See `memory/MEMORY.md`
  ("THE FY2023 PIVOT").
- **Past board chair of Gallatin Shalom Zone**, the same nonprofit where IDB administrator
  Preston Stark now sits as Treasurer — she rolled off the board roughly a year before
  2026-08, around when she was consolidating her GEDA directorship.
- **Publicly credited the Gallatin daycare funding package** — $500K+ IDB, $150K+ Shalom Zone,
  $500K state grant, over $1M total, to "Gallatin Daycare Centers." A May 2024 WKRN
  appearance (sourced secondhand via a pasted Google AI session, not yet independently
  verified) has her giving an earlier, different breakdown — $550K IDB ("administrative and
  maintenance fees"), $110K Shalom Zone, $500K ARPA — publicly pledged while the named
  beneficiary corporation was administratively dissolved by the state. Full knot:
  `memory/MEMORY.md` (Shalom Zone / Gallatin Daycare entries), `memory/TIMELINE.md`
  `#bates` `#gdc` `#shalom-zone`.
